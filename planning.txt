Hidhaya AI — Final Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Product Name

Hidhaya AI
Your Personal Islamic Companion

1. Vision

Hidhaya AI is an advanced AI-powered Islamic guidance platform designed to provide authentic, emotionally supportive, and easy-to-understand Islamic answers using Quran and Hadith references.

The platform allows users to ask questions naturally in:

English
Hindi
Urdu
Bengali
Roman Urdu

Example Questions:

“Sabr kya hai?”
“Iman kise kehte hain?”
“How to control anger in Islam?”
“Depression me kya dua padhe?”
“Shirk kya hai?”
“Bachon ki tarbiyah kaise karein?”

The core mission is:

“Make authentic Islamic knowledge simple, trustworthy, emotional, and accessible for everyone.”

2. Main Goals

The platform must:

✅ Use Quran and Hadith references from the provided dataset only
✅ Give emotionally supportive and simplified answers
✅ Support multilingual understanding
✅ Avoid hallucinated Islamic rulings
✅ Build trust through authentic references
✅ Handle semantic Islamic understanding intelligently
✅ Scale for millions of Quran and Hadith records in future

3. Core AI Philosophy

Hidhaya AI is NOT a Mufti or scholar.

The AI should:

Explain Islam simply
Use only provided references
Never invent Hadith or Quran references
Never generate fake authenticity grades
Never give unsupported fatwas
Encourage users respectfully
Say “Please consult a qualified Islamic scholar” when confidence is low

8. Core AI System Architecture
IMPORTANT

The AI must NEVER directly answer from general knowledge first.

Correct AI Flow
Step 1 — User Question

Example:

“Sabr, Iman aur Taqwa kya hai?”

Step 2 — Query Normalization

The system removes noise words:

Examples:

Input	Normalized
“Iman kise kehte hai Islam me?”	iman
“What is shirk?”	shirk
“Bachon ki tarbiyah kaise kare?”	tarbiyah
Step 3 — Cross-Language Semantic Understanding

The system understands meaning across languages.

Examples:

User Input	Topic
dhairya	patience
sabr	patience
patience	patience
गुस्सा	anger
maafi	forgiveness
tawakkul	trust_allah
shirk	shirk
Step 4 — Strong Search Pipeline
4-Layer Search System
Layer 1 — Exact Matching

Find exact phrase matches.

Layer 2 — Relaxed Word Matching

Partial word matching.

Layer 3 — Semantic Topic Expansion

Example:

shirk →
polytheism
idolatry
associating partners with Allah
false worship
Layer 4 — Fuzzy Matching

Misspellings supported:

sherk → shirk
imaan → iman
gussa → anger

STRICT RULES
